The *_s.png, *_n.png and *_sss.png maps in this directory are the built-in baseline material maps
of NVIDIA's RTX Remix for Minecraft (https://github.com/NVIDIAGameWorks/rtx-remix-minecraft,
src/main/resources/assets/minecraft/textures/block), renamed to Minecraft 1.12 texture names.
